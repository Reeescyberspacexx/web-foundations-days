About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you capture ideas instantly before they disappear. It streamlines your daily productivity by keeping your thoughts, tasks, and reminders organized in one accessible place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought into the input box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to create the note.
  3. Review your saved notes under the list section anytime.

Features

  • Fast and simple text note entry
  • Semantic structure for seamless navigation
  • Clean layout built with standard HTML5 elements
